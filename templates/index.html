GCS Docket Report

Shift Date (covers 08:00 that day to 08:00 the next day)

Logs into GCS and pulls every docket booked in this shift window, plus any older backlog docket that got cleared during it. Reporter 99412764 is excluded from the main Dockets/CPAN view; their CPAN-DL dockets land in Other, MAAN-TL dockets land in MAAN. Sheets: Final Report (matches the reference DAILY_CPAN_DOCKET_CALCULATION sheet, fully automated), Dockets, CPAN, MAAN, Other, and Summary (booked/cleared/pending breakdowns plus per-staff average clear-time KPI). Hover over a person's ID to see their name. The Final Report also breaks "Total Resolved" into today's-booking vs old-backlog sub-rows, so the day/night-duty figures reconcile clearly. Reporter 99412764's DL-down/TL-down dockets are additionally excluded from the Final Report's own day/night-duty and pending calculation (GCS mis-records their Date Submitted as 00:00 hrs regardless of actual booking time) - they still appear normally in Other/MAAN. Takes ~1 min per ~100 dockets.
